What we buy

If it's new, returned, discontinued or just taking up space, we'll make an offer on the whole lot. We don't cherry-pick the best SKUs and leave you with the rest.

Overstock and closeouts

Discontinued SKUs, seasonal leftovers, packaging changes and over-orders still in the case.

Customer returns

Tested or untested, single-retailer or mixed. We price for the real condition, whatever the return rate.

Shelf pulls

Goods pulled from stores with minor shelf wear, price stickers or dented boxes.

Damaged and salvage

Freight damage, insurance claims, and water or smoke salvage. Unsellable units are recycled with paperwork.

Surplus equipment

Restaurant lines, office furniture, forklifts, warehouse racking and light industrial machinery.

Store fixtures

Shelving, gondolas, displays and point-of-sale hardware from remodels and closures.

Not sure it qualifies? Send us a photo. Most answers come back the same day.

From dock to paid in a week

No auctions, no consignment, no waiting to see what sells. We buy outright and take on the resale risk ourselves.

  1. Send what you have

    A manifest, a spreadsheet, or a few photos of the pallets. Whatever you've got is enough to start.

  2. Get a firm offer

    Within 48 hours, in writing, and good for 14 days. The number doesn't change at pickup.

  3. We pick it up

    Our trucks or our carriers, scheduled around your dock hours. Loading and freight are on us.

  4. Get paid

    ACH or wire transfer on the day the goods leave your building.

Questions we hear a lot

Something else on your mind? Call us. A real person picks up during warehouse hours.

What's the smallest amount you'll buy?

One full pallet. For equipment and fixtures there's no minimum. A single commercial range or a run of shelving is worth a call. There's no upper limit; we regularly take multiple truckloads from one site.

How do you decide what to offer?

We price each lot on what the specific goods will resell for, their condition, and how quickly that category moves. It isn't a flat percentage of retail, so a lot of current-season electronics will get a very different offer from a lot of last year's seasonal decor.

Do you take untested returns or damaged goods?

Yes. Tell us roughly what you know about condition and we'll price it accordingly. Anything we can't resell is recycled or disposed of responsibly, and we can provide documentation for your records.

Can you keep our products off certain sales channels?

Yes. We can remove labels and tags, restrict resale to export or wholesale only, or keep goods off specific marketplaces. These restrictions are written into the bill of sale.

Who pays for freight?

We do, for accepted offers anywhere in the continental US. We schedule pickup around your dock and bring our own labor when needed.

How do buyers pay for lots?

ACH or wire transfer before pickup. Established buyers can apply for net terms. You can pick up from our warehouse or we can quote delivery.
